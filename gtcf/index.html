About the Foundation

One entity, many arms, and until now no page that says so.

The Foundation has served Auburn since 1977. GTCF Ltd is a single registered charity, ABN 18 044 027 709, and "Auburn Gallipoli Mosque" is a registered business name of that same ABN. The mosque, the aged care home, the schools, the fitness centre and the funeral fund all sit under it.

GTCF's DGR endorsement covers one fund only, the Gallipoli Mosque Scholarship Fund, effective 5 August 2025. Writing "tax deductible" on a renovation or zekat gift would be a false representation, so the receipt wording is built into the fund itself rather than left to whoever writes the page.

14

Today the mosque runs two competing donation pages with no fund receipting and no ABN, and sharing the link on WhatsApp previews as JotForm's logo with the words "Please click the link to complete this form".

How it is built

A$5 to A$12 a month, for both sites.

  • StackAstro on Cloudflare, Sveltia CMS, Humanitix, HubSpot Free, GiveNow, Cal.com.
  • BuildPhase 1a, the GTCF site: about four to six days.
  • LaterThe member portal bolts on at roughly A$47 a month without re-platforming anything.
15

The same git push pipeline already runs two other sites, so there is no new hosting bill and no builder subscription. Doing the member portal inside HubSpot instead would cost roughly A$1,400 a month plus a mandatory onboarding fee.
